# Gates

Vehicle and personnel access control points.

## Relationships

- Fed by [[Air Force Base]] power (barriers, ID scanners, cameras)
- Supported by [[SecFo]] (staffing)
- Part of [[Perimeter]] and [[Air Force Base]]

## Natural Disaster Vulnerabilities

- [[Ice Storm]] — Disrupts — barrier mechanism freeze
- [[Flood]] — Interdicts — road inundation at gate approaches

## Cyber Vulnerabilities

- [[Hacking]] — Compromises — access control database manipulation
- [[Social Engineering]] — Compromises — credential forgery, impersonation
