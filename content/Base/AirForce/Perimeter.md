# Perimeter

Fence line, sensors (motion, IR, camera), and lighting defining the installation boundary.

## Relationships

- Fed by [[Air Force Base]] power (sensors, lighting)
- Backed up by [[Backup Generation]] (priority sensors only)
- Supported by [[SecFo]]
- Part of [[Air Force Base]]

## Natural Disaster Vulnerabilities

- [[Hurricane]] — Disrupts — fence breach, sensor damage, lighting loss
- [[Flood]] — Disrupts — sensor inundation, erosion under fence
- [[Ice Storm]] — Disrupts — camera/sensor icing

## Cyber Vulnerabilities

- [[Hacking]] — Compromises — camera feed interception, sensor spoofing
- [[Social Engineering]] — Compromises — tailgating, credential cloning
